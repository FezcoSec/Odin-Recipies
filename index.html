Koshary Home

Koshary is a traditional egyptian recipe which comprised of some delicious stuff, it's a well known dish in egypt which is served in a lot of places and could be bought by any one in the society rich or poor due to it's price

Ingredients

  • Lentils
  • Rice
  • Tomato sauce
  • Macaroni
  • Daka
  • Onion rings

Steps to make this dish

  1. Put macaroni first
  2. Then cover it with a good amount of lentils
  3. After that put the rice on the top of them
  4. Then put some tomato sauce and daka sauce at last
